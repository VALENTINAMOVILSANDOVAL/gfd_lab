# 🌊 Geophysical Fluid Dynamics (GFD) Lab 🌍

## 📖 Overview
When we study fluid dynamics, we are interested in understanding how fluids move and evolve over time. But what exactly is **Geophysical Fluid Dynamics (GFD)**? GFD is the application of fluid dynamics to naturally occurring fluids on Earth and other planets, such as the atmosphere ☁️ and the oceans 🌊. Since these fluids are part of the Earth system, they are called *geophysical fluids*. 

By studying their motion, we can better understand important phenomena such as weather patterns 🌪️, ocean currents 🚤, and climate 🌡️. Two key features that make geophysical fluids unique are the **rotation of the Earth** 🌀, which affects fluid motion, and **stratification** 🥞, which refers to the layering of fluids with different properties. 

In this series of notebooks 📓, we will introduce the main concepts of GFD, combining physical intuition, mathematical derivations 🧮, and simple examples to help build a solid understanding of the subject.

## 🗂️ Repository Contents

The notebooks in this lab are structured to guide you from foundational equations through complex wave dynamics and instabilities. 

### 🛠️ Foundations & Tools
* **`tools.ipynb`** 🔧: Introduction to the mathematical and computational tools used throughout the lab.
* **`goveqns.ipynb`** ⚖️: Derivation and physical intuition of the governing equations (mass continuity, momentum, and thermodynamics).
* **`shallow.ipynb`** 💧: Exploration of the shallow water equations as a simplified model for geophysical flows.

### 🌎 Planetary Dynamics
* **`globe.ipynb`** 🌐: Fluid dynamics on a rotating sphere, exploring the effects of planetary geometry.
* **`equator.ipynb`** 🌴: Equatorial dynamics, including the equatorial beta-plane and trapped waves.

### 🧠 Core Theoretical Frameworks
* **`pv.ipynb`** 🔄: Understanding Potential Vorticity (PV) and its conservation as a fundamental tracer.
* **`quasigeostrophic.ipynb`** 📉: The Quasi-Geostrophic (QG) approximation for large-scale, mid-latitude flows.

### 🌊 Waves, Shear, and Instabilities
* **`rossby.ipynb`** 〰️: The mechanics and propagation of Rossby waves.
* **`shear.ipynb`** ✂️: Analysis of shear flows and their impacts on fluid behavior.
* **`barotropic.ipynb`** 🌀: Barotropic fluids and the mechanisms of barotropic instability.
* **`baroclinic.ipynb`** ⛈️: Baroclinic instability, the primary mechanism driving mid-latitude weather systems.
* **`ssmodes.ipynb`** 🧩: Specialized study of specific structural modes within the fluid.
